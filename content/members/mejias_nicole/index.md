Nicole is a student pursuing a major in Biostatistics and Health Data Science in the College of Health at Lehigh University. Her current research focuses on applying functional data analysis methods to influenza forecasting and analysis including epidemic curve modeling and Functional Principal Components Analysis (FPCA).

Nicole's interests include computational health science, statistical modeling, infectious disease forecasting, sports medicine research, athlete rehabilitation, and data-driven approaches to improve human performance and health. Her long-term goal is to pursue a career in computational health research, applying statistical and data science methods to public health, sports medicine, biomechanics, prosthetics, and athlete rehabilitation.
